## Intent

Second cut of the Jev inbox-triage animation (v1: `animation/inbox-triage.mp4`, 32s). Shorter, hook-first, better motion. All data comes from the real run in `results/raw.json`; nothing is invented.

Structure:
1. 0 to 2.7s, hook: the toddler email (e09) enters, tags land, `safety 0.98`, verdict "Human now".
2. 2.7 to 5.4s: the disguised developer pitch (e17) is archived.
3. 5.4 to 10s: the remaining 18 emails sort quickly into four lanes; counters fill.
4. 10 to 15s: focus pull into the result card: 19/20, 0 risky emails automated, 338 ms median, about 2.5 cents a month at 600 emails.

## Customizations

- Look: Apple dark-mode design language (user request, replaces the v1 palette): true black, Inter as SF stand-in, system colours, Mail-style cards, widget tiles, keynote stat slide. Previous look kept at `../_backups/inbox-triage-15s.v2-dark.html`.
- Count-up treatment on result stats (counting-dynamic-scale).
- Focus-pull transition from the sort to the result.
- Audio: sound effects on cards and motion (user request): notification, click-soft, ping, pop, whoosh-short, whoosh, chime from the bundled Pixabay-licensed library (`assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`). No music bed. Cues generated by `build-data.py` from the same schedule as the visuals.
